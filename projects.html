2026

Post-Quantum Secure Federated Learning System

A 20-client federated learning system that trains an image classifier on wildlife camera-trap data without centralizing it. Model updates are secured with NIST-standardized post-quantum cryptography and benchmarked against a classical X25519/Ed25519 baseline across 10 emulated network conditions. Published as a sole-author paper at ISC Türkiye 2026.

  • Federated learning
  • ML-KEM
  • ML-DSA
  • AES-256-GCM
  • MobileNetV2

2026

Storm the Forecast

An XGBoost pipeline that forecasts air temperature at four horizons, from 16 hours to 5 days, using 21 meteorological variables and 166 engineered features. Validation MAE was 1.81°C at 16 hours and 3.20°C at 5 days.

  • XGBoost
  • Time series
  • scikit-learn
2026

Deep Reinforcement Learning Agent for the Isolation Game

A dueling Double DQN trained by self-play on a custom 7×7 Isolation environment, combined with heuristic playouts. It won 88% of 1,000 games and runs in a Pygame human-versus-AI interface.

  • Reinforcement learning
  • Dueling DQN
  • Pygame
2025

SKYN

My undergraduate thesis, a mobile app for early detection of five skin diseases using fine-tuned CNNs trained on 16,549 images, with over 88% accuracy. It has a FastAPI inference backend, Firebase for authentication and storage, and a Flutter interface with separate roles for patients and medical professionals.

  • CNN
  • Flutter
  • FastAPI
  • Firebase
